# Image credits

Photos come from two sources. Most are CC0 or Public Domain Mark (PDM) and were found through [Openverse](https://openverse.org); the id column holds the Openverse id. The hemp pre-roll and hemp flower photos, the hemp pre-rolls and hemp flower category tiles, the grinder and the rolling papers photo are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which allows free commercial use; the id column holds the Unsplash photo id. Attribution is not required by either licence, but it is given here anyway. Files live in `public/images/` and were resized, cropped and stripped of metadata with ImageMagick. Most Openverse sources were only available at about 1024 px wide (rawpixel and stocksnap cap their free sizes), so the 1200 px product squares and 1600 × 1200 banners are upscaled from smaller originals. Unsplash sources were downloaded at 2000 px wide. The descriptions below are ours, not the photographers' titles. The dried-flower photos are described neutrally ("dried flower", not "hemp flower") because the sources could not be confirmed to show hemp; the product alt text uses the same wording. The eight strain photos in `products/hemp-flower/` stand for the strain, not the batch: each one was checked for smoke, lit or burning material, people, pipes, labels, watermarks and shop branding before use, and photos of living plants were rejected so every file shows dried flower.

The glassware photos (`products/glassware/` and the `categories/glassware.jpg` tile) and the brand tincture bottles in `products/tinctures/` are a deliberate exception to Addendum A photo rule 6: they are manufacturer or retailer product shots that show brand labels and the pieces themselves, used to sell those exact products. The id column holds the source product slug. Permission has to be confirmed with each brand, or the photo replaced, before launch. THC branding, cannabis leaves, watermarks, other shops' logos, smoke and people are still not allowed, and three products were dropped for showing a leaf (see the glassware and brand tinctures spec).

The payment-method marks in `public/images/payments/` are not photos: six are brand marks from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) released under CC0 1.0 and are shipped unmodified (single path, brand colour, 24 × 24 viewBox); the id column holds the Simple Icons slug. `card.svg` is our own generic credit-card drawing, used because "Pay With Card" must not carry Visa, Mastercard or Amex marks, which are licensed separately. Chime has no freely licensed mark, so it renders as a text badge and has no file here. See `public/images/payments/README.md` before adding another one.
